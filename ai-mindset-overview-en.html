HOW TO ENTER A DEPARTMENT · PAIN FIRST, TOOL LATER
01
Listen

Their pain, their words. Ask what wastes their week. Never open with AI.

02
Show

45-minute demo on their real data within two weeks. Seeing beats slides.

03
Co-create

Build the pilot with their people, one measurable goal, 90 days max.

04
Hand over

The champion runs it and presents the win. You support. They own the story.
