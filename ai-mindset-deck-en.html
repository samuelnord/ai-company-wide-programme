Company-wide AI Programme · Business ↔ IT · 2026

From AI tools to an AI mindset — in 12 months

A service-manager approach: identify, evaluate and implement AI use cases with every department — and anchor AI sustainably through change management, enablement and training.

01 · The role

The interface between business and IT

THE ROLE OWNS
  • The use-case funnel: identify → evaluate → implement
  • Change management, enablement & training
  • Guardrails, champion network & round table
BUSINESS OWNS
  • Use cases, data & domain knowledge
  • The pilot goal and its metric
  • Adoption — the story is theirs
IT OWNS
  • Platform, integration & security
  • Approved tool operation
  • Data access & architecture

A service manager, not a gatekeeper: both sides get supported — business articulates the need, IT delivers safely, the role translates and keeps momentum. Culture sticks when every department has its own win to defend.

02 · The 12 months

Four phases, one ticket

M1–2

01 IDENTIFY

Listening tour through all departments, business and IT together. Pain first, no tech. Output: use-case backlog.

M3–5

02 EVALUATE & PILOT

Backlog ranked by value ≥ effort. 3 lighthouse pilots, ≤ 90 days, one metric each. Champions appointed, guardrails published.

M6–9

03 IMPLEMENT

Monthly decision-only round table. AI academy runs. Proven pilots become copyable playbooks.

M10–12

04 ANCHOR

AI objective in every department plan. Lightweight governance board. Ideas flow bottom-up.

03 · Approaching departments

The conversation playbook: pain first, tool later

  • 01Listen — "What eats your week?" Sit in their meetings, walk the line. Never open with AI.
  • 02Show — within 2 weeks, a 45-minute demo on their real data. Seeing their own RFQ answered beats any slide.
  • 03Co-create — pilot built with their people, one measurable goal, 90 days. They name it.
  • 04Hand over — the champion presents the result to leadership. You sit in the audience.

Do

Speak their KPIs (scrap rate, OTD, quote turnaround) · celebrate small wins loudly · make trying safe.

Don't

Lead with tools or hype · promise headcount savings · centralise every decision · punish failed experiments.

04 · Culture mechanics

How the mindset actually shifts

Champions network

One volunteer per department, 20% protected time, monthly exchange. They translate — you don't scale, they do.

Visible quick wins

Every pilot ≤ 90 days with one number attached. Wins are shown at town halls — by the team, not the strategist.

Safe to try

Weekly open "AI hour", sandbox with approved tools, no-blame reviews of failed experiments. Failure is data.

Rule of thumb: after month 6, more use-case ideas should come from departments than from the service manager. That's the signal culture is moving.

05 · Guardrails

Governed, not slowed — the EU AI Act reality for a manufacturer

  • Most factory & office use cases are minimal/limited risk — copilots, drafting, inspection support: transparency duties (live since Aug 2026), no heavy conformity work.
  • The high-risk lane is narrow but real — HR screening & people analytics fall under Annex III: obligations bind 2 Dec 2027. Design human-decides workflows now.
  • Three artefacts cover 80% — an AI use policy, an approved-tool list, and role-based training (Art. 4 AI literacy — already a duty).
  • One register, every system — purpose, owner, risk tier, data. No entry, no rollout.
06 · Measuring

KPIs that prove mindset, not just usage

ADOPTION

% of staff using approved AI tools weekly. Target: 60% by M12.

CAPABILITY

% trained per role tier. Target: 80% basics, 100% of champions advanced.

VALUE

Live use cases per department (≥1) and hours saved / quality gains per pilot.

MOMENTUM

Bottom-up ideas per quarter — the culture metric. Should overtake top-down by M6.

Reported monthly on one page at the round table. No dashboards nobody reads.

07 · Asks

What this needs to start

  • 2One champion per department — volunteer, 20% time protected by their lead.
  • 3A small pilot budget — 3 pilots × 90 days; tooling and time, not consultants.
  • 4One hour per month — the decision-only round table with department leads.
