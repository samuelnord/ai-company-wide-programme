01 · WHY

Why this law matters to you

New law
The AI Act

The European Union has a new law for AI. The short name is the AI Act. Parts of the law are active now. More parts start later.

Risk
Up to €35M

If your company breaks the law, you can pay a large fine. The fine can reach €35 million. It can also reach 7% of your global revenue.

Benefit
Speed, with safety

Good AI governance is not only a rule to follow. It helps your teams use AI faster. It also lowers your risk.

This guide gives you the facts you need now. You do not need a legal background to use it.

02 · WHAT

What the AI Act says

The AI Act sorts every AI system into one of four risk levels. Each level has different rules.

Risk levelExampleRule
BannedSocial scoring, hidden manipulationForbidden now, since February 2025.
High riskHiring tools, credit checks, exam gradingStrict rules. They start in December 2027 and August 2028.
Limited riskChatbots, AI images, deepfakesYou must label the content as AI. Live now.
Minimal riskSpam filters, spell checkersThe AI Act sets no extra rules here.

Key dates: 2024, the law starts. 2025, bans and staff-training duties start. 2026, labelling rules start. 2027 and 2028, high-risk rules start.

03 · HOW

How to follow the law: five steps

  • 1
    List your AI systems
    Write down every tool that uses AI. Include tools your teams already use today.
  • 2
    Sort each system
    Give each system a risk level from the table on the previous page.
  • 3
    Write one AI policy
    Keep it short. State what staff can do, and what they cannot do.
  • 4
    Train your staff
    The law requires this. Training also lowers your risk, because trained staff catch mistakes early.
  • 5
    Review often
    Check each system again when you change the tool, the data, or the users.
